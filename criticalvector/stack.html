The Stack

The sites, podcasts, and databases that actually get used. Curated FOAM and reference resources across emergency medicine, critical care, anesthesiology, and toxicology.

Emergency Medicine + Critical Care
⚡
Life in the Fast Lane
litfl.com — The gold standard for EM and critical care FOAM. ECG library, toxicology, clinical cases.
 🔴
RebelEM
rebelem.com — Evidence-based emergency medicine with honest appraisal of the literature.
 🧠
EMCrit
emcrit.org — Scott Weingart on resuscitation, critical care, and the hard cases.
 📡
The Poison Review
Medical toxicology and FOAM. Useful when the patient's medication list is the problem.
