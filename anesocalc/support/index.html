AnesCalc

Privacy Policy · Effective August 2, 2026

Summary

AnesCalc does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All information entered into the app remains on your device.

Data Stored Locally

Patient demographics (age, weight, height, sex, hemoglobin, and perioperative parameters) are saved locally on your device using iOS system storage so they persist between sessions. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to the developer or any third party.

Location

AnesCalc requests access to your device's location solely to determine altitude. Altitude affects MAC (Minimum Alveolar Concentration) calculations for volatile anesthetics. Location access is requested only when you explicitly tap the reticle button in the app. The app requests When In Use permission only — it does not access location in the background. No location data is stored beyond the current session, and none is transmitted off-device.

No Tracking or Analytics

AnesCalc contains no advertising, no analytics SDKs, no crash reporting services, and no third-party frameworks that collect data. There is no tracking of any kind.

No Account Required

AnesCalc does not require an account, login, or any form of registration.

Children

AnesCalc is intended for use by healthcare professionals and medical students. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised effective date.
