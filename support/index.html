What AnesCalc Does

AnesCalc is a weight-based dosing calculator and clinical reference tool for anesthesia providers. It calculates drug doses across 10+ categories from a single set of patient demographics, and includes 10 clinical reference tools, drug mechanism cards, unit conversions, and a vasopressor infusion rate calculator.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my patient data stored or transmitted?
No. All patient data is stored locally on your device using iOS system storage. Nothing is transmitted to any server.

Why does the app request location access?
Location is used solely to detect altitude, which affects MAC (Minimum Alveolar Concentration) calculations for volatile anesthetics. The app requests When In Use access only, and only when you tap the reticle button. No location data is stored or transmitted.

Is AnesCalc a replacement for clinical judgment?
No. AnesCalc is a reference and calculation tool intended to support — not replace — provider judgment. Always verify dosing with your institution's protocols and supervising anesthesiologist.

Which devices are supported?
AnesCalc requires iOS 17 or later and supports iPhone and iPad.
