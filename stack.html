The Stack

The sites, podcasts, guidelines and databases that actually get used. Curated FOAM and reference resources across emergency medicine, critical care, anesthesiology, ultrasound, toxicology and transport.

Emergency Medicine
⚡
Life in the Fast Lane
litfl.com · ECG library, toxicology, eponyms and clinical cases. The first stop for EM and critical care FOAM.
 🔴
REBEL EM
rebelem.com · Evidence-based emergency medicine with an honest read of the literature.
 📝
First10EM
first10em.com · Justin Morgenstern's monthly paper reviews and evidence deep dives.
 🏥
St Emlyn's
stemlynsblog.org · UK emergency and critical care blog and podcast focused on evidence and clinical judgment.
 🎙️
EM Cases
emergencymedicinecases.com · Anton Helman's round-table podcast with expert panels, show notes and quizzes.
 📚
emDocs
emdocs.net · Practical reviews by emergency physicians, with curated FOAM resource series.
 💡
ALiEM
aliem.com · Academic Life in EM. Clinical posts, the AIR series of vetted blog reviews, and education tools.
 🫀
Taming the SRU
tamingthesru.com · University of Cincinnati EM: shock and resuscitation, clinical guidelines, EMS material.
 🎧
EM:RAP
emrap.org · Monthly audio, CorePendium and board review. Subscription; free for EMRA student and resident members.
 🎯
EMRA
emra.org · Emergency Medicine Residents' Association. Student resources, guides and EM:RAP access for members.
Critical Care
🧠
EMCrit
emcrit.org · Scott Weingart on resuscitation, airway and the sickest patients in the department.
 🫁
PulmCrit
emcrit.org/pulmcrit · Josh Farkas on ICU physiology, pharmacology and practice-changing trials.
 📖
Internet Book of Critical Care
emcrit.org/ibcc · Farkas's free, continually revised ICU textbook, organized by organ system.
 ⚗️
Deranged Physiology
derangedphysiology.com · ICU physiology and pharmacology explained in depth. Written for exam study, not bedside use.
 📊
Critical Care Reviews
criticalcarereviews.com · Trial summaries, annual books and investigator interviews on the biggest ICU trials.
Anesthesiology + Regional
💉
OpenAnesthesia
openanesthesia.org · Free anesthesiology education from the International Anesthesia Research Society.
 🎧
ACCRAC
accrac.com · Anesthesia and Critical Care Reviews and Commentary. Jed Wolpaw's podcast from Johns Hopkins.
 🔬
NYSORA
nysora.com · Regional anesthesia techniques, sonoanatomy and the Nerve Blocks app. Some content is paid.
 🧭
ASRA Pain Medicine
asra.com · Regional anesthesia and pain guidelines, including the LAST checklist and anticoagulation guidance.
 🛡️
APSF
apsf.org · Anesthesia Patient Safety Foundation newsletters and safety priorities.
Ultrasound
🖼️
The POCUS Atlas
thepocusatlas.com · Open-access image library by organ system, plus an evidence atlas of test characteristics.
 🎓
Madtown Sono
madtownsono.emed.wisc.edu · University of Wisconsin EM ultrasound lectures: RUSH, eFAST, DVT, nerve blocks and MSK.
 ⏱️
5 Minute Sono
coreultrasound.com · Short videos on single applications, from knobology to tamponade and thoracentesis.
Toxicology
📡
The Poison Review
thepoisonreview.com · Medical toxicology news and literature. Useful when the medication list is the problem.
 🐕
Tox & Hound
emcrit.org/toxhound · Medical toxicologists on the controversies the literature buries.
 ⚖️
ACMT
acmt.net · American College of Medical Toxicology position statements and guidance.
Prehospital + Transport
🚁
FlightBridgeED
flightbridgeed.com · Critical care transport education and podcast; FP-C and CCP-C preparation.
 🚑
NAEMSP
naemsp.org · National Association of EMS Physicians position statements and Prehospital Emergency Care.
Guidelines We Cite
📋
ACEP Clinical Policies
acep.org · Emergency department clinical policies from the American College of Emergency Physicians.
 🩸
Surviving Sepsis Campaign
sccm.org · SCCM and ESICM adult sepsis guidelines.
 ❤️
AHA CPR & ECC Guidelines
cpr.heart.org · American Heart Association resuscitation guidelines: BLS, ACLS, PALS and neonatal.
 🌡️
MHAUS
mhaus.org · Malignant hyperthermia protocols and the 24-hour MH hotline.
